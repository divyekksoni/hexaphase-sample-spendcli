# Vision: SpendCLI

## Problem
Expense apps are slow and need accounts; terminal users want a 2-second, offline way to log spending.

## Goal
A command-line tool to log personal expenses and see monthly summaries.

## Target users
Developers and terminal users who want fast, offline expense tracking
